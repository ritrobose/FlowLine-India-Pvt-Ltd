Company Profile

Since 2001, Flowline India Pvt. Ltd. has been at the forefront of delivering innovative, high-performance industrial ventilation and air movement solutions. With over two decades of engineering excellence, we have earned the trust of industries across India by manufacturing products that combine reliability, efficiency, and long-term performance.

PRODUCT PORTFOLIO

Our Solutions

Our comprehensive product portfolio includes Belt Driven Centrifugal Fans, Direct Driven Centrifugal Fans, Fan Filter Units (FFUs), Axial Flow Fans, Air Curtains, Air Scrubbers, HVLS Fans, and a wide range of customized ventilation solutions tailored to meet the evolving needs of modern industries. Backed by a state-of-the-art manufacturing facility equipped with advanced machinery, precision engineering equipment, and modern production technologies, every product is crafted using premium-grade materials and stringent quality standards to ensure exceptional performance, durability, energy efficiency, and reliable operation in demanding industrial environments. With dedicated manufacturing, quality control, research, assembly, and logistics departments working in complete synergy, and a continuous focus on upgrading our infrastructure with the latest industry advancements, we consistently deliver high-quality solutions that meet both national and international standards while ensuring efficient production and timely delivery.
